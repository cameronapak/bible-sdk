Render AO Lab Free Use Bible API passages the way AO Lab's own reader lays them out. Words no longer run together where the text splits around words of Jesus or footnotes ("him, “Most certainly"), and Chinese and Japanese text gets no added spaces. Poetry lines stay whole across footnotes, verse numbers hang in the poetry indent, headings come before Psalm titles, and paragraph breaks show a consistent gap. A Psalm title now appears only with the verse it introduces. Plain text from the data SDK follows the same lines and spacing.
